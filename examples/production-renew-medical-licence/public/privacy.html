Privacy

This page says what this service does with your information.

What we use

  • your Trident ID number or National Registration Number, to find your record
  • your name, parish, email, phone and registration details, which we look up so you do not have to type them
  • where you practise
  • the CPD records you upload
  • your payment reference

Why we use it

We use it to renew your registration with the Medical Council of Barbados, and to send you your certificate. We do not use it for anything else.

The full privacy notice

This service is in alpha. The full privacy notice is being written with the Medical Council. It will say how long we keep your information, who we share it with and what your rights are. We will publish it on this page before the service opens to the public.

Questions

If you have a question about your information, contact us.
