Help with renewing your licence

What you need

  • your Trident ID number or National Registration Number
  • your CPD records for this year, as PDFs or photos
  • a way to pay the renewal fee

If you cannot finish in one go

Choose “Save and come back” on any page. We will email you a link to carry on from where you stopped.

If your details are wrong

You can correct your email, phone, parish or postal address on the “Is this you?” page. If the page shows someone else’s details, stop and call the Medical Council on 246-535-7400.

If you are stuck

You can call the Medical Council on 246-535-7400. You can also see other ways to get in touch.
