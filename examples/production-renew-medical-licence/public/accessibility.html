Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to renew their licence here. That includes people who use a screen reader, a keyboard only, voice control or a magnified screen.

How accessible this service is

We are building this service to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1.

The service is in alpha. It has not yet had a full accessibility audit, and it has not yet been tested with people who use assistive technology. Some parts may not work for you.

Every step works without JavaScript.

If you have a problem

Tell us what did not work. Say which page you were on and what you were using.

If you cannot use this service, call the Medical Council on 246-535-7400 to renew another way.
