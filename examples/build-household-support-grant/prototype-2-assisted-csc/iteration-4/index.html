Help someone apply for the Household Support Grant

The Household Support Grant pays K 1,500 a month to households that need help with money. It is paid on the 5th of each month.

You run this service on the CSC's certified device. The citizen sits beside you. Read the boxes marked "Read aloud" out loud to them.

Before you start

  • The citizen is here in person, or a person acting for them is.
  • You use your own name on this device. It is recorded on every page.
  • SPA never asks for a PIN. You never ask for one. Not for a wallet, not for a bank.
Does the citizen have a KestriaID? A card or a number. If they are not sure, choose No. We will use another way.

Are you a village digital champion?

You can help someone get ready and book a visit. You cannot sign anyone in, look up their records, or act for them. You are not an authorised intermediary. A CSC officer does that.

The Social Protection Agency will now look up some details about your household in government records. We will look up:

  • the district and village where you live, not your full address
  • who is in your household, and when SPA last visited
  • any support you already get from SPA
  • SPA's own check of whether your household can probably get the grant. We do not get a score.
  • where a payment to you would go: the name of the bank or wallet, and the last 4 digits

We do this because running this grant is part of SPA's job. The law is the Data Protection Act 2022, section 13. We are telling you. We are not asking for your permission.

We will not look up your health, tax, land or vehicles. Every lookup is listed under "Who has used my data" in your GovKestria account. You can ask to see your records and to correct them.

    Question map

    • Name: known (KestriaID claims). No KestriaID: ask.
    • District and village: known (address, National Population Register), shown as district and village only. Missing or wrong: ask, then flag for a survey.
    • Household members, last survey, support already got: known (household, programmes, National Social Registry via KesLink). No record: book a survey visit.
    • Outcome: derived by SPA. We get hsgAssessment only. The welfare band is never shown.
    • Who receives the money: ask. Any adult member. Never an authorised intermediary.
    • How the money is paid: known (payoutAccount, provider and last 4 digits). A CSC can change the recipient and the route at level 3. It never edits an account.
    • Changes, safe contact number, declaration: ask.
    • Not pulled or asked: date of birth, sex, KUIN, TIN, vehicle, tax, land, health, disability.

    Resolved (round 1)

    Settled by the Kestria profile and the service sheet. This is profile evidence, not user evidence. No user has tested this prototype.

    • Resolved What an intermediary can do. They can apply and report changes. They cannot name themselves recipient or change the route. The record is made at a CSC and renewed every 12 months. Champions are not intermediaries. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, "Who can apply, and who gets paid" (reg.7).
    • Resolved Outcomes and messages. Four hsgAssessment outcomes, each with its exact message, replace our own demo eligibility. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, "What an application can lead to".
    • Resolved Amount and timings. K 1,500 a month on the 5th; about 4 weeks; 8 to 10 weeks with a survey; up to 4 more weeks in the rainy season (March to June) in the Northern Plains and Lake Region; first payment at least 7 days after the decision. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, "What it is" and "Timings"; world.md.
    • Resolved Declaration wording and penalty. Fixed wording, a plain declaration needing no particular assurance level (Electronic Transactions Act 2020, s.6). Source: services/household-support-grant.md, "How people can apply".
    • Resolved Reference format. HSG- plus 7 digits. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, "Messages". The booking reference (BK-) is still invented.
    • Resolved How a payout change is made at a CSC. A CSC changes the recipient and the route, never an account. Account changes happen at the citizen's own provider. Source: dpi.md, Payouts.
    • Resolved How another recipient's money is found. In order: account linked to their KUIN, their phone number, a cash voucher. Source: dpi.md, Payouts.
    • Resolved Districts, members and bands in PERSONA_EXTRAS. Deleted. The structured values come from the personas. Source: dpi.md, Value shapes and Mock configuration.
    • Resolved Phone number on record. It is in the personas' claims (not Aru's). Source: dpi.md, Mock configuration.
    • Resolved Village committee still confirms households. Confirmation, then a survey visit, then a cash voucher. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, sign-in table, "No KUIN". What the confirmation looks like is still open (above).
    • Resolved A card check is an allowed alternative to a fingerprint for applying and reporting a change. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, sign-in table. The level number is still open (above).
    • Resolved Progress check. *999# or the helpline. Our made-up "reply STATUS" is gone. Source: services/household-support-grant.md, "Messages".
