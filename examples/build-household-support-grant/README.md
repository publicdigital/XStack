# Build – Apply for the Household Support Grant

> **Kestria profile example. Kestria is a fictional country.** This build uses `profiles/kestria/` to show what xstack does with a government running on MOSIP and eSignet, Mojaloop and X-Road. It also tests the profile: see `profile-findings.md`.

- **Generated by:** the `xstack:brief-to-prototypes` skill, via `/xstack:build`
- **Brief source:** `brief.md`. This is a synthetic demo brief made from the profile's world bible, because Kestria has no real discovery.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Status:** three candidate prototypes, clicked through end to end in a browser. Not tested with users, because none exist.

> These prototypes are throwaway alpha. Every person, number and institution in them is fictional.

## The three hypotheses

| # | Name | Hypothesis | Strongest for | Weakest for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Once-only, phone-first** (`prototype-1-once-only/`) | The citizen signs in with KestriaID. We pull their household, programmes and payout account, and they confirm rather than retype. They see the outcome before applying. | Smartphone owners already in the registry whose KestriaID phone number works | Shared phones, people not in the registry |
| 2 | **Assisted at a Citizen Service Centre** (`prototype-2-assisted-csc/`) | A CSC officer runs the service, and the citizen signs in by fingerprint after a separate consent step. Village champions prepare and refer. Not being in the registry becomes a booked survey visit. | No smartphone, old phone number, low literacy, Vell and Sora speakers, no KUIN | People far from a CSC, especially in the rainy season |
| 3 | **Household-first** (`prototype-3-household-first/`) | Any adult tells SPA what's changed in the household and who should get the money. The default outcome is a reassessment or visit. | Households whose record is out of date. The person who looks after the money. | People who just want a yes or no |

The service designer replaced the original third idea, a proactive SMS offer. It was the same bet as Prototype 1 with a different way in, it gave only the lowest level of sign-in, and it left out households not in the registry. Why each decision was made is in `build-spec.md`.

## How to open them

Open any `index.html` in a browser. There's no server and nothing is loaded from the internet. When KestriaID asks who to sign in as, pick a test person – each one runs a different scenario (see `test-plan.md`). Press "Show assumptions" in the black xstack banner to see what the prototype is assuming.

All three have the same chrome, so the test is about the journey:
- a "Kestria is a fictional country" banner
- the GovKestria header with a language switcher
- the alpha banner
- the xstack banner
- the footer with the CSC finder

They use the xstack neutral style because Kestria has no design system yet.

## Iteration 2 (2026-10-23)

Each prototype now has `iteration-2/` with `index.html`, `CHANGES.md` and `assumptions.md`. The feedback is `feedback-round-1.md` in each prototype folder. It came from internal critique and the fixed Kestria profile – **not from users**, so no `[VERIFY WITH USERS]` assumption is resolved.

**What changed in all three:**
- `PERSONA_EXTRAS` is gone. Each prototype uses `KESTRIA_PERSONAS` from `profiles/kestria/dpi.md`, filtered by the service sheet. This was checked: the persona data is identical to the profile's.
- The outcome comes from `hsgAssessment`, so Mirel now gets "probably can't".
- They use the service sheet's rules, declaration, text wording, timings and payment date.

**What changed in each:**
- **P1 and P2:** who receives the money now comes before the payout route.
- **P2:**
  - the officer can change the recipient and the route, never an account
  - Aru is rescued after the simulated fingerprint check
  - the CSC is in the citizen's own district
  - an intermediary can't be the recipient or change the route
  - the switch from the Older Persons Grant is offered
- **P3:**
  - whether the person is the registered person comes from the data (Tessa)
  - reporting a change can lead to a survey visit, as the rules say
  - all 38 districts are listed

**What was checked:**
- The JavaScript passes `node --check`.
- The DPI mock is unchanged.
- Each prototype was clicked through in a browser:
  - P1: as Ines, Mirel and Desa
  - P2: as Aru, and as Anaïs with Mina acting for her
  - P3: as Tessa, reporting a new baby

  Errors showed correctly along the way.

**Not checked:**
- every other route
- a screen reader
- the 320px layout of iteration 2
