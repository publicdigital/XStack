Apply for the Household Support Grant

The Household Support Grant is a monthly payment from the Social Protection Agency (SPA). It helps households that are struggling with money.

Use this service to find out if you can get it, and to apply. It takes about 10 minutes.

What you need

  • Your phone, with the KestriaID app on it, if you have one
  • A phone number where we can send you text messages

Using a phone you share? We show no personal details until you sign in. Close this page when you finish.

Got a text message about this? Check that the web address says gov.kestria.test/hsg. We will never ask for your PIN.

Do you have a KestriaID?

 A KestriaID is the digital ID that goes with your national ID card. You do not need to know your ID number. Select an answer. Choose yes if you have a KestriaID. Choose no or not sure if you don't.

You can still apply without a KestriaID

You do not need a KestriaID to get help. There are three ways to apply.

Answer questions here

We ask a few questions. Then SPA arranges a visit to your household. A visit means a decision takes about 8 to 10 weeks.

Go to a Citizen Service Centre

An officer can sign you in with your fingerprint and help you apply. They can also fix an old phone number on your record.

Find your nearest Citizen Service Centre

Call or dial

Call the free line 0800 400 400. Or dial *999#.

If you have the KestriaID app on your phone, try signing in. It is quicker.

How we will use your details

You are signed in as .

To check if your household can get the grant, SPA will get these facts from government records:

  • the district where you live
  • who is in your household
  • any support you already get from SPA
  • which account we would pay into (only the provider and the last 4 digits)

We will not get your tax, land, vehicle or health details.

Why we do this

So you do not have to type what government already knows. You will check each fact before we use it.

The law lets SPA use these records for this job (Data Protection Act 2022, section 13). That is why we are not asking you to agree. You can see every time we use your details. Look for "Who has used my data" in your GovKestria account.

    Question map

    • Name: known, KestriaID claims. Fallback: ask (page "Your name and phone number").
    • Phone: known, KestriaID claims. Confirmed, with a safe-number fallback.
    • District: known, address from the National Population Register. Fallback: ask, and flag for a survey.
    • Household members: known, household from the National Social Registry via KesLink. Fallback: survey request.
    • Already getting SPA support: known, programmes. Unavailable: do not block.
    • Eligibility: derived. Band and programmes. Never shown.
    • Nearest CSC: derived from district.
    • Changes since last survey: ask.
    • Where we pay: known, payoutAccount, provider and last 4 only. Change = CSC at level 3.
    • Who receives the money: ask.
    • Declaration: ask.
    • Not pulled: date of birth, sex, KUIN, TIN, vehicle, tax, land, health. Disability and health are not asked.

    Assumptions

    • Verify with users People with a smartphone, a working KestriaID phone number and the wallet app can sign in at level 2 and finish in about 10 minutes.
    • Verify with users The start page works as the landing page for a neutral SPA text message, and people trust it.
    • Verify with users Asking "Do you have a KestriaID?" first does not make people without one feel like second-class applicants.
    • Verify with users "I'm not sure" is a common answer. The no-KestriaID page helps those people decide.
    • Verify with department "About 10 minutes" is realistic. The happy path is 11 pages, not the "about 6" in the brief. Testing should show which pages could merge.
    • Verify with policy Eligibility is: household in the National Social Registry, lowest welfare bands, and no other SPA cash programme. This is a demo assumption. It is not in the profile.
    • Verify with policy Showing only the outcome ("probably", "we need to visit", "you already get support") is allowed, and "probably" does not create a legal expectation.
    • Verify with department The grant is K 1,500 a month, paid monthly. A decision takes about 4 weeks from a complete online application, or 8 to 10 weeks if a survey visit is needed.
    • Verify with policy SPA's pull is public task (Data Protection Act 2022, s.13), so it needs a notice, not a consent request. Every pull appears in "Who has used my data".
    • Verify with users People read the data-use notice and understand the plain-language version of the legal basis.
    • Verify with platform SPA is an X-Road subsystem that can call its own National Social Registry for household and programmes, and a MyData record is written for each pull.
    • Verify with platform payoutAccount comes from the Treasury disbursement service (KesPay account lookup), not KesLink. The prototype treats all four attributes as one KesLink pull.
    • Verify with platform Level 2 sign-in (wallet app linked) is enough to apply for benefits, as dpi.md says.
    • Verify with data About 17% of phone numbers on record no longer work. The service always asks the person to confirm the number, and offers another.
    • Verify with policy It is lawful to show the names of other household members, including children, to the signed-in applicant. The prototype shows names and "adult" or "child" only, not dates of birth.
    • Verify with users People can tell if the household list is right. "Someone is missing" leads to a survey visit.
    • Verify with department Any reported change (birth, death, move, lost income) means a survey visit, not a quick update. The registry has self-updated since 2025, so a lighter route may exist.
    • Verify with users People will report changes honestly, and understand "lost work or income" without a definition.
    • Verify with department Any active SPA cash programme stops a new application, and an unavailable programmes lookup never blocks one. SPA checks in the back office.
    • Verify with department A person who says the account is wrong can carry on and change it at a CSC before the first payment. We hold the application meanwhile.
    • Verify with department With no linked account, the fallback is the phone number (mobile money), then cash at a post office with a one-time code. Nothing in the profile says how cash works for someone with no KUIN.
    • Verify with platform If another adult receives the money, the Treasury service can find where to pay them (their KUIN or phone). The profile does not say how.
    • Verify with users The person who looks after the money is often not the phone owner. This variant texts the applicant's phone, so that person may still not see the message.
    • Verify with policy A tick-box declaration is valid (Electronic Transactions Act 2020, s.6). A statutory declaration needs level 2 or above (s.8). Without KestriaID, the declaration may need repeating at the visit.
    • Verify with policy The wording about paying money back for false information is invented.
    • Verify with department People can check progress by dialling *999# or by replying to the text with their reference. No SMS short code exists in the profile.
    • Verify with data The reference is HSG- plus 7 digits, made up at random. It is fake.
    • Verify with data The page works at 320px and on 2G or 3G. Not yet tested on a real phone. Only inline code: no fonts, images or frameworks.
    • Verify with users The Back link is left off the confirmation page on purpose, so nobody sends the application twice.
    • Verify with department The nearest Citizen Service Centre can be worked out from the district. The profile has no list of CSCs, so the prototype only links to a stub page.

    Known gaps

    • Known gap Payouts: the mock has no payout contract, because dpi.payments.create takes money in. The payout pages are ordinary service pages with fake data. The payments mock is never called.
    • Known gap Consent screen: dpi.data.fetch always shows a consent screen, but SPA's pull is a notice. A notice page comes first. "No" is treated as "carry on with manual answers and a survey". Pressing Escape on the mock screen returns to the notice.
    • Known gap Assurance level: the mock always returns substantial (level 2). Level 3 (CSC fingerprint) is only described on the payout change page.
    • Known gap Languages: only English works. Kestrian, Vell and Sora show a notice. The profile has no text in those languages. The law requires Kestrian and English (Digital Government Act 2023, s.14).
    • Known gap No Kestria design system. This uses the xstack neutral style.
    • Known gap The mock persona data has no phone number, no household members and no welfare band. They come from a separate PERSONA_EXTRAS object in the page script. It is fake and keyed by persona.
    • Known gap The mock address is one free-text string. The district is read from it by splitting on commas. A real register would return fields.
    • Known gap The mock consent screen lists "Home address", but this service only keeps the district.
    • Known gap No test persona has a partial result (address, support or payout account missing but a household found). The researcher controls below simulate that.
    • Known gap The mock sign-in claims give one fullName. The profile says KestriaID returns given_name and family_name. The name is split on the last space for the manual page.
    • Known gap Some stub pages (privacy, accessibility, find a CSC) are not built.

    Security must-nots for this variant

    • Never ask for a PIN, a full account number, or a KUIN that sign-in already gave us. Never show a raw score or band.
    • Before sign-in the page shows no names, household details or amounts. The start page says "a monthly payment" and gives no amount.
    • No "you qualify" wording on any page that could reach a shared phone. The text message is neutral: reference and *999# only.
    • Payout account changes go to a CSC at level 3. Nothing is edited inline. We text the old number when it changes.
    • "We will never ask for your PIN" is on the payout pages and the confirmation page.
    • Every route works without a KUIN. The start page asks "Do you have a KestriaID?".
