Tell us about your household

Use this service to tell the Social Protection Agency (SPA) about your household. This includes anything that has changed, like a birth, a death, or someone moving in or out.

SPA uses what you tell us to check what help your household can get. This includes the Household Support Grant, which is K 1,500 a month.

We can’t say yes or no straight away. SPA checks your household first, and may visit.

Before you start

  • Anyone aged 18 or over in the household can do this.
  • You do not need a KestriaID.
  • It takes about 10 minutes.
  • If you share a phone, close this page when you have finished.

How we will use your household details

SPA will now look up your household record. We are allowed to do this by law, to run SPA’s programmes (Data Protection Act 2022, s.13). So we are telling you, not asking for consent.

What we will look up

  • the people in your household, and your household number
  • the district your household lives in
  • support your household already gets from SPA
  • the name of the provider and the last 4 digits of the account we would pay into

What we will not look up

Your health, disability, tax, land or vehicles.

You can see every lookup in “Who has used my data” in your GovKestria account.

If you would rather answer yourself, you can. We will then visit your household to check the details.

The bet

Start from “what has changed in your household?”, not “apply”. Anyone aged 18 or over in the household can start. The default outcome is a reassessment or a survey visit, not a yes or no on the spot.

Question map

  • Name: known, KestriaID claims. Fallback: ask on the contact page.
  • Phone to contact: ask. The mock’s claims have no phone (see gaps).
  • District: known, National Population Register. Fallback: ask, and flag for a survey.
  • Household members: known, National Social Registry via KesLink. Fallback: how many people live there, then a visit.
  • Already getting SPA support: known, National Social Registry. Missing: don’t block; SPA checks in the back office.
  • Eligibility: derived. Never shown. No band, no score.
  • What changed: ask (add-another).
  • Who receives the money: ask.
  • Where we pay: known, provider and last 4 digits only. Changing it: CSC, level 3, never inline.
  • Declaration: ask.
  • Not pulled: date of birth, sex, KUIN, TIN, vehicle, tax, land, health. Not asked: disability or health.

Assumptions

  • Verify with users Households find “tell us what has changed” easier to start than “apply”.
  • Verify with users Adults know whether the household is registered under them. “I’m not sure” catches the rest.
  • Verify with users The adult who looks after the money will start this, and will find “who should receive the money” a normal question.
  • Verify with users A short, neutral text to the registered phone is enough, and does not put the person filling this in at risk on a shared phone.
  • Verify with users People hear “a visit” as normal checking, not as an accusation.
  • Verify with users People will report a death or lost income in a short form, without a person beside them. The copy is gentle and asks nothing about health.
  • Verify with users “About 10 minutes” is right on a slow phone with a data bundle.
  • Verify with users The household’s own names and ages shown after sign-in feel safe, not exposing, on a shared phone.
  • Verify with policy Another adult in the household may see the other members’ names and ages after signing in at level 2.
  • Verify with policy Telling SPA about household changes as another adult is not “acting for someone else” (level 3 in dpi.md). This prototype allows it at level 2 and blocks any payout account change.
  • Verify with policy An adult can name the payee without the registered person agreeing. Only a neutral text goes to the registered phone: “SPA: a household record was updated. Dial *999# for details.”
  • Verify with policy The declaration is a plain statement, not a statutory declaration. A statutory declaration needs KestriaID level 2 or above (Electronic Transactions Act 2020, s.8), which the no-KestriaID route cannot give.
  • Verify with policy Eligibility: in the National Social Registry in the lowest welfare bands, and not getting another SPA cash programme. This is a demo assumption. The service never shows the band or says “you qualify”.
  • Verify with policy Recording a payee’s name from someone with no KUIN, with no identity check until the visit, is acceptable under National Identity Act 2020, s.19.
  • Verify with department Decision times: about 4 weeks from a complete online application, 8 to 10 weeks if a survey visit is needed.
  • Verify with department The default outcome is a visit. In this prototype a visit follows if any change is reported, if the person is not sure, if there is no record, or if the last survey was 4 or more years ago. Otherwise: a record check in about 4 weeks. The “last survey” years are fake data.
  • Verify with department A household that already gets the grant can report changes here, and these go to the team that looks after payments. No timing is promised.
  • Verify with department SPA can pay an adult who is not the registered person, and can find that person’s account from their own KUIN, then phone number, then post office cash.
  • Verify with department A request with no KestriaID and no household number goes to the district office’s survey queue. District office staff can match a district to a visit.
  • Verify with platform The KesLink household lookup returns the household for any adult member’s KestriaID, not only the registered person’s.
  • Verify with platform The Civil Registration Office already tells SPA about births and deaths. We ask anyway because the record may lag, and we do not pull birthRecord.
  • Verify with data “Check progress” works through a *999# menu and by replying STATUS. The profile gives no SMS short code and no USSD menu.
  • Known gap Payouts. The mock has no payout contract, because dpi.payments.create takes money in. It is not called. The payout page is an ordinary service page with fake data markers.
  • Known gap Consent screen. dpi.data.fetch always shows a yes/no consent screen, but SPA’s pull is public task (s.13), so it should be a notice. A notice page comes first. “No” on the mock screen is treated as “carry on with manual answers and a survey”.
  • Known gap Assurance level. The mock always returns level “substantial” (2). Changing a payout account needs level 3, so that is sent to a CSC and never done here.
  • Known gap Phone number. The mock’s claims have no phone number, so we ask for a contact number instead of confirming the one on record.
  • Known gap Address and household shape. The mock’s address is a full street address and household is one string. Districts, member lists, the registered person and last-survey years come from a separate PERSONA_EXTRAS object, which is fake data.
  • Known gap Signing in as a non-registered adult. The mock can only sign in its 7 test people. Only Ines Kestel is a non-registered adult. The role question is not checked against KesLink.
  • Known gap Districts. The profile lists 23 of the 38 districts. The district list here is incomplete, so there is a “my district is not on this list” option.
  • Known gap Languages. The language switcher shows Kestrian, English, Vell and Sora. Only English works. The profile has no Kestrian, Vell or Sora text. Digital Government Act 2023, s.14 needs Kestrian and English.
  • Known gap No Back link on the confirmation page. Going back would suggest the answers can be unsent. A departure from the build spec.

Security must-nots built in

  • No names, household details or amounts before sign-in. The start, role and KestriaID pages are neutral.
  • Never asks for a PIN, a full account number or a KUIN. “We will never ask for your PIN” is on the payout page and the confirmation page.
  • Never shows a band or a score. No “you qualify” wording anywhere. The only text to the registered phone is neutral.
  • The payout account is shown as provider and last 4 digits. Changing it goes to a CSC at level 3, never inline. The old number is texted when it changes. Nobody acting for the household can change it.
  • Every route works without a KUIN. The question comes right after the role question.
  • “You’re telling us about [household]” and who is filling it in are shown on every page after the household step.
