xstack prototype: Register a SIM card – Version 1

Register your SIM card

Every SIM card must be registered to the person who uses it. Register yours by 31 March 2027.

After that date, the network will switch off SIM cards that are not registered. You will not be able to call, text, use data or mobile money.

It is free. It takes about 5 minutes.

What you need

  • Your KestriaID
  • The phone with the SIM card in it, so you can get a text message

You can register up to 5 SIM cards. A parent or guardian registers SIM cards for children under 16.

Using a phone you share? Register the SIM card in the name of the person who uses it most. Close this page when you finish.

Got a text message about this? Check the web address says gov.kestria.test/sim. We never ask for your PIN.

Register at a network shop

Without a KestriaID, you can register your SIM card at a Lumo Telecom or Kora Mobile shop.

Take one of these:

  • your passport
  • your residence permit
  • your birth certificate and a letter from your village committee

For a child under 16

Children cannot register a SIM card. A parent or guardian registers it in their own name.

They need to register it themselves

A SIM card used by someone aged 16 or over must be registered to their own KestriaID.

Registering a SIM card in someone else's name without their agreement is an offence.

They can register it on this service, or at a Lumo Telecom or Kora Mobile shop.
