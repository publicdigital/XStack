Get a fishing licence

You need a licence to fish in rivers and lakes. It costs $40 for 12 months or $10 for 1 month. It's half price if you're 65 or over.

If you sign in with National ID, we'll use details the government already has, so you won't need to type them.

Question map

  • Name, date of birth: known, from National ID. Fallback: ask.
  • Address: known, from the Civil Registry via the data exchange. Confirmed, not prefilled. Fallback: ask.
  • Half-price concession: derived from date of birth. Never asked.
  • Licence length: ask.

Assumptions

  • Verify with platform National ID returns name and date of birth at an assurance level this service accepts.
  • Verify with platform The Civil Registry address is reachable through the data exchange for this purpose.
  • Verify with policy There is a lawful basis to pull the address with the citizen's consent.
  • Verify with users People read pulled details before confirming, and notice an old address.
  • Verify with users People who continue without National ID don't feel it's a lesser route.
  • Verify with department Fisheries offices can take payment with a reference when card payment fails.
